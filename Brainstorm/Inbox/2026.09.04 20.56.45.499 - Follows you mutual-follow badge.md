# "Follows you" mutual-follow badge

Kategori: ux | Efor: S | Rank: 30

## Ne
Add `bool FollowsViewer` to the `FollowStatusResponse` record in MainProject/Backend/SocialGraphApiContracts.cs (currently `(bool IsFollowing, int FollowerCount, int FollowingCount)`). In FollowEndpoints.cs's `BuildStatusAsync` helper, compute it with the reverse-direction call that already exists on the interface: `viewerPublicId is not null && await follows.IsFollowingAsync(targetPublicId, viewerPublicId)` — no new store method needed, IFollowStore.IsFollowingAsync just gets called with the arguments swapped. In UserProfile.razor, render a small badge/chip next to `<p class="profile-identity">` when `_followStatus.FollowsViewer && !IsOwnProfile` (e.g. `<CoreChip Text="Follows you" />`, the same component Profile.razor already uses for the copy-feedback chip).

## Neden
Right now the follow status only tells you if you follow them, not whether they follow you back — the single most common piece of context every mainstream social app shows on a profile, and it costs one boolean plus one reused store call, nothing new to build.
