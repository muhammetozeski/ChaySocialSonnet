# Following-filtered feed tab on Home

Kategori: ux | Efor: M | Rank: 20

## Ne
Add a two-tab toggle above the Feed section in MainProject/UI/Pages/Home.razor: 'For You' (current GetRecentPostsAsync behavior, unchanged) and 'Following' (posts only from people the viewer follows). Add `Task<IReadOnlyList<string>> GetFollowingIdsAsync(string publicId)` to MainProject/Backend/IFollowStore.cs; implement trivially in ChaySocialSonnet.Web/Backend/LocalFollowStore.cs by returning `followingByFollower.GetOrAdd(publicId, ...).Keys.ToList()` (the dictionary already exists, just isn't exposed). Add `GET /api/follow/{publicId}/following-ids` to ChaySocialSonnet.Web/Backend/FollowEndpoints.cs and a matching `GetFollowingIdsAsync` on MainProject/Services/FollowApiClient.cs. In Home.razor, when the 'Following' tab is selected, call FollowApi.GetFollowingIdsAsync(ViewerPublicId), then PostApi.GetPostsByAuthorAsync(id, RecentPostsCount, ViewerPublicId) per followed id, merge and sort by CreatedAt descending into a separate `_followingPosts` field, and render that list instead of `_posts`. Add tab styling to MainProject/UI/Pages/HomeCss.razor.

## Neden
Right now Home.razor only ever shows the global recent-posts firehose — following someone (via UserProfile.razor's Follow button) has no visible effect on the feed itself, so the follow graph doesn't change what you see day to day. This makes following functionally meaningful without touching any shipped feature.
