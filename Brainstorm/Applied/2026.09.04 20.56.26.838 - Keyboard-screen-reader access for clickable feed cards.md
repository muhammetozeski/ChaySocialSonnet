# Keyboard/screen-reader access for clickable feed cards

Kategori: a11y | Efor: S | Rank: 30

## Ne
CoreCard.razor (MainProject/UI/Elements/Core/CoreCard.razor) renders its clickable variant as a plain <div class="core-card clickable" @onclick="HandleClick">, with no tabindex, no role, and no keyboard handler. Add: when OnClick.HasDelegate, emit role="button" tabindex="0" and an @onkeydown handler that invokes HandleClick on Enter or Space (preventDefault on Space so the page doesn't scroll), plus a visible :focus-visible outline in CoreCardCss.razor. This single fix flows through GlassCard (which wraps CoreCard) to every existing clickable card without touching call sites: Search.razor's '.search-result' rows (line 21, opens a profile) and Notifications.razor's '.notification-row' rows (line 21, opens a post or profile) both become tab-reachable and Enter/Space-activatable for the first time.

## Neden
Right now a keyboard-only or screen-reader user literally cannot open a search result or a notification — the only two ways into those flows are pointer clicks on an unfocusable div. Fixing it once in CoreCard covers both existing screens and any future clickable card for free.
