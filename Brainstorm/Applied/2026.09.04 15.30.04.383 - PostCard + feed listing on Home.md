# PostCard + feed listing on Home

Kategori: feed | Efor: M | Rank: 10

## Ne
Add a PostCard.razor component wrapping GlassCard (showing the first few characters of the author's PublicId + the Text + a relative time for CreatedAt). Home.razor currently still carries a completely empty "template-container" placeholder ("Welcome to your new Hybrid App! auauauua") and HeroFeedPanel is not used anywhere - MainProject/UI/Panels/HeroFeedPanel.razor.cs (the parameter class/code-behind) exists but the corresponding HeroFeedPanel.razor markup file is not in the repo. So this task also covers actually switching Home.razor over to HeroFeedPanel and writing the markup for the panel's Hero/Intro/Feed slots; the Feed slot will pour the result of IPostStore.GetRecentPostsAsync(count) into a list of PostCards using PageBase's existing loading-state structure (and show a proper "no posts yet" state for the empty-list case too).

## Neden
IPostStore/LocalPostStore stands as a working, tested backend (registered in DI at Program.cs:29, verified by MainProject.Tests/Backend/LocalPostStoreTests.cs) but no screen calls GetRecentPostsAsync. Looked at through the "Feed" lens, this is the feature's most basic gap: the "wall" view itself. It uses the existing GlassCard/PageBase architecture as-is, doesn't invent a new design pattern, and is an increment verifiable on its own in the browser even without the composer (it becomes functional including the empty-state) - whereas adding the composer without feed listing would leave a half-feature where the user can't see their posted post anywhere, unverifiable in the browser.
